Connected Spaces

Curated by Angela Bottazzi

Curatorial Statement

This exhibition showcases a theme of networked participation in early internet art, this focuses specifically on how digital systems can allow multiple people to shape a single artwork. Rather than treating this “connection” as a broad concept, the works I included in this exhibition I hope serve to highlight a more specific idea that collaboration as the structure of the artwork itself.

In these pieces, the audience is no longer something passive. Instead, the viewers become contributors whose actions can directly influence form, narrative, and meaning. From real time telecommunication experiments to interactive storytelling and collective archives, each work helps to demonstrate a different model of shared authorship.

What connects these works is not just the use of the internet, but the way each one depends on participation as well in order to exist. The network is not only a medium between the two, but it becomes the material of the artwork itself.
